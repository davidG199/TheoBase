## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Philosophy
The design system embodies the intellectual rigor, objective clarity, and structural discipline of Neo-Swiss International typography combined with contemporary data journalism. Rooted in the visual legacy of Massimo Vignelli and Karl Gerstner, the aesthetic rejects sentimental ornamentation, religious skeuomorphism, and soft decorative treatments. Instead, it positions historical and theological documentation as an architectural, high-precision archive.

The system communicates authority, historic permanence, and mathematical exactitude. Visual weight is determined by temporal and analytical hierarchy rather than decorative embellishment. The atmosphere is that of an illuminated gallery archive: restrained, scholarly, and uncompromisingly deliberate.

### Design Movement: Neo-Swiss International & Editorial Data Journalism
- **Strict Structural Grids:** Mathematical column-and-lane structures order all information, making temporal correlation immediately readable.
- **Asymmetric Tension:** Vast contrasts between colossal structural typography and micro-scaled tabular data points create dynamic visual momentum.
- **Ink-on-Paper Tactility:** Hard offsets, stark borders, and an unyielding lack of blurred lighting yield a tactile, physical press sensibility.
- **Chromatic Rigor:** Color is never decorative. It functions purely as a semantic data-encoding tool against an archival substrate.

## Colors

### Structural Roles
The palette is governed by archival print constraints. The interface operates in an architectural light mode:

- **Canvas Neutral (`#F4F4F0`):** The primary gallery surface. A warm, unbleached paper tone that relieves eye fatigue across dense historical analysis and prevents stark digital glare.
- **Ink Primary (`#111111`):** High-density carbon ink used for primary body copy, bold titles, structural lane boundaries, and hard offset drop shadows.
- **Grid Lines (`#E0E0E0`):** Fine architectural registration rules that define coordinate lanes and spatial columns without visual noise.
- **Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved exclusively for elevated focus cards, flyout drawers, search matrices, and interactive hovering nodes.

### Categorical & Semantic Data Palette
Color application is strictly deterministic. Colors must not be mixed or applied ornamentally:
- **Prophetic Teal (`#008080`):** Prophecy, covenants, spiritual declarations, and exegetical messengers.
- **Conflict Crimson (`#D72638`):** War, conflict, martyrdom, imperial campaigns, and blood sacrifices.
- **Sovereign Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Monarchies, temple construction, dynastic reigns, and imperial decrees.
- **World Violet (`#663399`):** Extra-biblical secular empires, Greco-Roman synchronous events, and global trade chronologies.

## Typography

### Typographic Rationale
The typographic system enforces clear demarcation between historical era, analytical body narrative, temporal coordinates, and scriptural quotation:

- **Primary Display (`Space Grotesk`):** Used for architectural era labels, structural headings, and modal search inputs. The bold, proportional letterforms ground the interface with mathematical precision. Large watermark headers are set with negative tracking and an ambient 10% opacity behind data lanes.
- **Analytical Body (`Inter`):** Delivers neutral, legible body copy for historical commentary, geopolitical contextual analysis, and drawer metadata.
- **Temporal & Data Layer (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to timeline markers, B.C./A.D. calendar coordinates, geographic coordinates, and statistical metrics.
- **Canonical Quotation (`Newsreader`):** Exclusively designated for verbatim scripture excerpts and ancient source citations within the context panel, immediately signaling an authentic, canonical voice.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture & Spatial Split
The system operates on an uncompromising 12-column grid system paired with strict vertical modular rhythm:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Full 12-column matrix with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. The canvas executes a dual-lane horizontal narrative:
  - **Biblical Narrative Lane:** Occupies the upper 60% of the active vertical canvas height.
  - **Global Context Lane:** Occupies the lower 40% of the active canvas height.
  - **Central Divider:** A continuous, unyielding `2px solid #111111` axis line anchors both lanes.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. The horizontal split adjusts to stacked synchronized lanes (upper Biblical tier, lower World History tier) with shared horizontal scrubbing.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters. The timeline pivots to a vertical sequential feed, maintaining the 2px left-hand ink boundary with parallel side-by-side node badges.

### Fixed Dimensions & Rhythm
- **Fixed Top Command Bar:** Exactly `64px` tall with a continuous `1px solid #111111` bottom border.
- **Fixed Bottom Scrubber:** Exactly `48px` tall set against a solid `#111111` ink ground with reversed text.
- **Context Drawer:** `480px` fixed width on desktop, locking to the full right canvas height (`100vh`) with a `2px solid #111111` separation.

## Elevation & Depth

### Hard Ink Elevation (Zero-Blur Philosophy)
In accordance with Neo-Swiss editorial standards, this system bans soft blurred drop-shadows, ambient feathering, and skeuomorphic light sources:

- **Sharp Cast Shadows:** All elevated surfaces project an absolute, un-blurred offset: `4px 4px 0px #111111`.
- **Active / Pressed State:** On direct pointer interaction, elevated cards shift coordinates directly (`translate(2px, 2px)`) while reducing shadow projection to `2px 2px 0px #111111`.
- **Selected Timeline Nodes:** Highlighted with a crisp `2px 2px 0px #111111` structural offset.
- **Framing & Containment:** Depth is established through crisp surface contrast. Elevated panels use `#FFFFFF` set against the `#F4F4F0` canvas, outlined by a razor-sharp `1px` or `2px solid #111111` ink perimeter.
- **Modals & Overlays:** The search matrix deploys a solid `95%` white ink wash overlay without blur filters, maintaining readable typographic grid coordinates underneath.

## Shapes

### Strict Geometric Hierarchy
The shape language is strictly architectural, relying on stark right angles (`0px`) juxtaposed against pure geometric circles:

- **Panels, Frames & Cards (`0px`):** All containers, context drawers, dropdown menus, and modular cards feature sharp 90-degree corners, reinforcing the print-register aesthetic.
- **Interactive Controls (`0px` to `4px`):** Buttons and form fields operate primarily with razor `0px` corners. Where tactile micro-differentiation is mandatory for primary actions, a maximum corner radius of `4px` is permitted.
- **Event Markers (`50%` Perfect Circles):** 
  - Major Era/Covenant Milestones: `24px` diameter circular nodes.
  - Minor Tactical/Localized Events: `12px` diameter circular nodes.
- **Dividers & Connectors:**
  - Macro Lane Separator: `2px` solid `#111111`.
  - Temporal Guides: `1px` solid `#E0E0E0`.
  - Synchronous Causal Connectors: `1.5px` vertical dashed ink paths linking synchronous secular events directly to biblical episodes.

## Components

### Buttons & Controls
- **Primary Action:** Sharp `0px` (or max `4px`) rectangular container, `#111111` background, `#F4F4F0` uppercase `Space Grotesk` bold text (`label-caps`), with a `4px 4px 0px` shadow in categorical accent color.
- **Secondary / Filter Pill:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #111111` border, hard shadow `2px 2px 0px #111111`. On active toggle, the fill swaps to the respective category color (`#008080`, `#D72638`, `#D4AF37`, or `#663399`) with white text.

### Timeline Event Nodes
- **Marker Construction:** Circular geometry filled with category color, bounded by a `2px solid #111111` ink ring.
- **Hover Micro-interaction:** Reveals a zero-radius archival tooltip rendered in `#FFFFFF`, with a `1px solid #111111` frame, `label-mono` date string, and `4px 4px 0px #111111` hard shadow.
- **Active State:** The clicked node pulses with a concentric hairline ring while anchoring the right-edge context drawer.

### Context Panel (Archival Drawer)
- Fixed at `480px` wide, slide-in from the right edge.
- Background in `#FFFFFF` with a `2px solid #111111` left border.
- **Sticky Header:** Displays canonical date (`JetBrains Mono`), major event title (`Space Grotesk`), and a minimal close icon (`24px` strike-cross).
- **Scripture Module:** Inset parchment block featuring `Newsreader` 18px text, framed with a left border accent in `#008080` or `#D4AF37`.

### Input Fields & Search Matrix
- **Global Search Field:** Flat `#FFFFFF` surface or borderless canvas integration, using large `headline-lg` input text. Underlined with a heavy `2px solid #111111` stroke.
- **Filter Matrix:** Three distinct columns (Eras, Figures, Events) aligned to the 12-column grid over a 95% opacity white overlay.

### Cards & Analytical Modules
- **Metric Cards:** `#FFFFFF` ground, `1px solid #111111` border, `4px 4px 0px #111111` hard shadow.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Category label in `label-caps` at top-left, hero metric in `Space Grotesk` (`headline-lg`), and explanatory note in `body-sm`.
